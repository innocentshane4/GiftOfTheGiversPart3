1. Introduction

This manual explains how to use the Gift of the Givers Donor Management Platform. The platform allows foundation staff, registered donors, and members of the public to:

  • Make donations in South African Rand (ZAR), US Dollars (USD), or Euros (EUR)
  • Choose once-off or recurring giving
  • Support a specific relief project or donate to the general fund
  • Download a tax certificate (Section 18A) for eligible donations
  • Register as a volunteer
  • Employees: post project updates visible to the public
Note No real payment is processed by this prototype. Donations are recorded for administrative and reporting purposes only.

3. Logging In

3.1 Employee Login

Employees are given login credentials by a system administrator. Use the Login link in the top-right corner of any page.

  1. Click Login in the navigation bar.
  2. Enter your work email address (e.g. employee@gotg.org) and your password.
  3. Click the blue Log In button.
  4. You will be redirected to the Employee Dashboard, which shows a summary of donations, active projects, and volunteer registrations.

Tip If you see "Access Denied" after logging in, your account has not been assigned the Employee role. Contact your system administrator.

3.2 Registered Donor Login

Donors who have created an account can log in to view their donation history and download tax certificates.

  1. Click Login in the navigation bar.
  2. Enter the email address and password you used when you registered.
  3. Click Log In.
  4. You will be taken to the Donor Dashboard, which shows your recent donations, tax certificates, and profile details.

If you do not yet have an account, click Register on the login page. Fill in your full name, email address, and a password that meets the complexity requirements shown on screen.

3.3 Anonymous / Guest Access

You do not need an account to make a donation or register as a volunteer. Simply visit the site and use the Donate or Volunteer links in the navigation bar. You will be asked to enter your name and email address directly on the form.

Note Anonymous donors cannot receive a tax certificate because the certificate must be linked to an identified donor on record.
4. Posting a Project Update (Employees)

Employees can post updates on active relief projects. Updates are visible to the public on the Projects page.

  1. Log in as an employee (see Section 3.1).
  2. From the Employee Dashboard, click Projects in the left-hand menu, or navigate to Employee Dashboard → Projects.
  3. Find the project you want to update in the list. Click its name to open the project detail page.
  4. Click the Post Update button.
  5. Fill in the Title and Content fields. The title is a short summary (e.g. "Week 3 Progress Report") and the content can be as detailed as needed.
  6. Click Save Update.
  7. The update will immediately appear on the public Projects page under the relevant project.

Tip You can view all updates for a project by clicking View Updates from the Projects list in your dashboard.
Important Project updates cannot be edited or deleted after posting. Check your content carefully before saving.
5. Making a Donation

To open the donation form, click Donate in the navigation bar or click any "Donate Now" button on the home or projects page.

Screenshot placeholder — Donate form (top half) Paste a screenshot of /Donations/Donate showing the amount options and currency selector.

5.1 Anonymous Donation

  1. Open the donation form.
  2. Select a preset amount (R50, R100, R250, R500) or type your own in the Custom amount field.
  3. Leave the currency as ZAR (default) or change it (see 5.4).
  4. Enter your full name and email address — these are captured for internal records only and will not be published.
  5. Tick the Make this donation anonymous checkbox.
  6. Optionally select a Relief Project from the dropdown, or leave it blank to support the general fund.
  7. Click Confirm Donation.
  8. A confirmation page will appear showing your unique reference number (e.g. GG-20261026-483927). Save this for your records.
Note — Anonymous vs Once-off An anonymous donation is processed as a once-off donation. Recurring donations require a registered and identified donor account. If you tick "anonymous" and also select "Recurring", the form will display an error.
Screenshot placeholder — Donation confirmation page Paste a screenshot of /Donations/Confirmation showing the reference number.

5.2 Once-off Identified Donation

  1. Open the donation form (log in first if you want the donation linked to your account, so you can download a tax certificate later).
  2. Select or enter a donation amount.
  3. Choose your currency.
  4. Enter your full name and email address.
  5. Leave the Make this donation anonymous checkbox unticked.
  6. Under Donation Type, select Once-off.
  7. Optionally choose a relief project.
  8. Click Confirm Donation and note your reference number.

5.3 Recurring Donation

A recurring donation requires a registered, logged-in donor account because the system needs to identify you for scheduled payments.

  1. Log in to your donor account (see Section 3.2).
  2. Open the donation form.
  3. Select or enter a donation amount.
  4. Under Donation Type, select Recurring.
  5. The anonymous checkbox will be greyed out — recurring donations must always be identified.
  6. Complete the remaining fields and click Confirm Donation.
Tip Registered donors can view all their recurring schedules from Donor Dashboard → My Donations.

5.4 Donating in a Different Currency

The platform supports three currencies: South African Rand (ZAR), US Dollars (USD), and Euros (EUR).

  1. Open the donation form.
  2. Click the Currency dropdown (default shows ZAR) and select USD or EUR.
  3. The preset amount buttons will remain in their numeric values; enter your desired amount in the Custom amount field.
  4. Complete the rest of the form as normal.

6. Generating and Downloading a Tax Certificate

Tax certificates (Section 18A) are available for identified, non-anonymous donations. You must be logged in as the donor who made the donation.

  1. Log in to your donor account.
  2. Click Donor Dashboard in the navigation bar and then select Tax Certificates from the menu.
  3. Your eligible donations are listed. Find the donation for which you need a certificate and click Generate Certificate next to it.
  4. The certificate is created (each donation generates exactly one certificate, so repeated clicks do not produce duplicates).
  5. A printable page will open showing the certificate number, donation amount, date, and donor details.
  6. Use your browser's print function (Ctrl + P on Windows or Cmd + P on Mac) to print or save the certificate as a PDF.

Important Anonymous donations are not eligible for a tax certificate. If you need a certificate, ensure you did not tick the anonymous checkbox when donating.

Employees can also view and access all tax certificates from Employee Dashboard → Tax Certificates.

7. Registering as a Volunteer

Anyone can register to volunteer with Gift of the Givers — no account is required.

  1. Click Volunteer in the navigation bar.
  2. Fill in the registration form:
    • Full Name
    • Email Address
    • Phone Number
    • Skills / Availability — describe what you can offer
    • Preferred Projects (optional)
  3. Tick the consent checkbox to confirm that you agree to being contacted by foundation staff.
  4. Click Submit Registration.
  5. A confirmation message will appear on screen. Foundation staff will contact you when opportunities that match your skills arise.

Tip — For employees New volunteer registrations appear in the Employee Dashboard → Volunteers list. You can view each registration to see contact details and availability.
8. Frequently Asked Questions
Question Answer
I forgot my password. What do I do? On the login page, click Forgot your password? and enter your email address. You will receive a reset link. If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder.
Can I donate without creating an account? Yes. You can make a once-off or anonymous donation without registering. You will not be able to download a tax certificate for an anonymous donation.
How do I stop a recurring donation? Log in, go to Donor Dashboard → My Donations, and contact foundation staff. Cancellation of recurring schedules is currently handled manually by an administrator.
Why can I not select Recurring and Anonymous at the same time? A recurring donation requires an identified donor so that scheduled contributions can be tracked. Anonymous recurring giving is therefore not supported.
I submitted a donation but did not receive a confirmation. Note down the URL in your browser after submission — it will contain your donation ID (e.g. /Donations/Confirmation/42). If the page did not load, contact foundation staff with the details of your donation.
Can a tax certificate be reissued? The same certificate is returned each time you click Generate Certificate for a given donation. It can be printed as many times as needed.
I cannot log in as an employee. Ensure you are using the credentials provided by your administrator. Employee accounts cannot be self-registered — they must be provisioned by an admin through the Admin Dashboard.
